1.0.0
First release.
The work bay is sized to each ship as it loads; a ship that fits leaves the bay stock.
Wide ships: both sides of the bay move apart to keep a set gap beside the ship, and the back wall and barge widen to match.
Long ships: the bay grows by 76 meters, with a second furnace and processor on each side and a longer barge.
Bay spotlights have range and brightness multipliers, and are raised further when the bay is widened.
Helmet light range and brightness multipliers, in the settings file.
Custom ships are straightened and centered, and the Master Jack and light jacks are moved clear of them.
The bay a ship was given is remembered, so a loaded save gets the same bay.
BAYS tab in the game's Options menu for every setting.
