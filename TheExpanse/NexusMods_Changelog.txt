Changelog for the TheExpanse page on NexusMods. One block per version: the first line is the version to
enter, the lines under it are the changelog lines.


0.8.1
Ammunition: each PDC has three crates of 2,400 rounds and needs its belts, feeds and receivers on board; the rail gun has 20 slugs in its loader crate and needs all its parts.
PDC squares sit around the reticle where each turret is on the ship, colored by rounds left; a red X marks a cut-off turret. AMMO counts under the reticles and on the wheel.
Rail gun fires when you let go of a full charge, with READY, CHARGING, FIRE and RELOADING shown in color; the sight sits on what the nose points at.
The small salvage stations around the work bay can be shot apart: pieces shatter with the game's cutter and drift away, the bay's frame stays standing.
Wrecked crates, frames and the stripped ship can spill real collectibles: credit drives most often, data drives, the bunny, truck parts, repair kits, oxygen and thruster fuel.
Green hexagons mark spilled loot from the seat and on the scanner's Objects screen, with distance and a count.
The nose view opens at 2x zoom and zooms up to 8x.
The stick that picked a wheel slice no longer moves the ship until it is let go.
The flip and burn's cancel hint shows the right key.
Quick sit for testing: F4 or both stick clicks put you in the nearest seat once the ship has loaded.

0.8.0
Weapon control from the pilot's seat: hold R / Y for the weapon wheel (the suit's tool wheel) or press 1-4: HELM, PDC, TORP, RAIL.
PDC: six turrets track the reticle when they can see it and fire tracers that spark and heat what they hit.
Torpedoes: the tube doors open in torpedo mode; each launch locks on the target, eases out, turns, burns and explodes on arrival; the trays empty as you fire.
Rail gun: charge and fire along the nose with the rail gate's flash and beam; the slug pierces several parts and the ship kicks back.
Flip and burn moved to G / D-pad left.
The flight HUD uses the suit's font with drop shadows, and its top rows are centered.
A ship touching a barge no longer stays highlighted red after the piece has left.

0.7.5
Pulling an injector's handle now always takes it off: its beam goes out and the screen updates. On the Rocinante the nozzle flaps kept it counted as connected.

0.7.4
Setup mode: fit any ship for flight. Demo charges mark the spots; Alt+1 RCS thruster blocks, Alt+2 drive plumes, Alt+3 a seat, Alt+Backspace removes.
Fittings are saved per ship class in ships.json (hand-editable) and come back on resumed saves.
RCS blocks fire their jets with the side thrusters and turning; ships can have several drive plumes.
The flight HUD shows the shift clock in the outside and nose views.
The Master Jack (the Hab) stays put and stops the ship like a wall; the Hab can be entered and the game saved after a flight.
Light jacks no longer bounce off the hull and slow to a stop.
The outside camera's pivot is right on resumed saves.
The reactor game runs only on the Expanse reactor (no pulsing core in other ships' small reactors).
The shutdown mode and order are picked anew for every ship spawned (they were the same every time) and kept when a save is reloaded, also after flying the ship.
Reloading a save with injectors already taken off carries on where you left off: they count as taken off, and with all of them off the reactor is cold.
Node Radiation slider in the options tab.
New option: Setup Mode in the EXPANSE tab.

0.7.3
Flight control: sit in any command chair on the MCRN Corvette and fly it out of the bay.
Side thrusters, turning, roll, main drive throttle, brake, hold attitude and flip and burn, on keyboard and mouse or controller.
Outside, nose and seat views; the outside view orbits and zooms.
Flight HUD: drive and thruster gauges, speed, prograde and retrograde bubble, turn rate, acceleration in g, flip and burn progress.
A thin hexagon marks the work bay and its distance in the outside views.
The drive plume burns with the throttle: a white-hot core with shock diamonds inside a blue sheath that fans out and tapers to a point.
Get up while under way: the ship stops where it is while you walk around and catches up when you sit down.
Artificial gravity toward the stern while the drive burns; loose things fall to the decks.
Deck boots: walk, run, crouch and jump on the decks under the drive's pull.
Your suit's HUD shows the Hab's distance, the ship's speed and its acceleration.
Room to fly: the work area's limits are lifted and the planet, Moon and Sun stay put in the sky.
The bay's jacks are let go when the ship first moves.
New options: Flight Control in the EXPANSE tab, and the [Flight], [Space] and [Drive] settings.
The reactor's fire flares are off while the ship flies (no more stray flames).

0.7.2
Three ways to shut the reactor down, picked at random per ship: Balance, Memorize and Resonance (each can be switched off in the options).
Memorize: the engineering deck terminals flash the order to take the injectors off in. Resonance: release the injector whose handle lights tick with the core's beat.
Two nav terminals on the engineering deck show the shutdown screen, with a pin marking where each one stands around the reactor.
The core pulses on a steady beat and shows its level by color: blue, orange, red, purple.
A wrong injector turns the core red and up a level; the right one brings it back down.
Handle lights: use the handle while its three lights are dark, or the plasma flashes back and sets you on fire.
Level 3: a ring of plasma destroys the injectors, then the reactor melts down. When it blows, the core detonates with it.
New options tab, EXPANSE: Live Reactor, escalation, meltdown, ship and player damage, and the three modes.
Shorter hitch when the ship spawns.

0.7.1
Reactor control on the MCRN Corvette: a ball of fusion plasma burns in the reactor chamber, held by the eight plasma injectors around it.
Each connected injector feeds the plasma through a beam; disconnecting one turns its beam off.
Disconnecting injectors out of balance makes the plasma pulse faster and larger and burst fire toward the gap.
Two gaps side by side make the plasma unstable: it sets you and the parts nearby on fire.
Badly unbalanced plasma melts down, the game's own reactor meltdown.
An injector is disconnected by pulling its handle or by cutting its purple pipe to the reactor's lid.
Pulling the reactor while the plasma burns sets off an explosion in the chamber.
New Reactor settings section.

0.7.0
Ghost ships are more haunted. The game's own haunting is kept and added to, and it all gets worse as nodes are destroyed.
The ship rolls slowly about its long axis now and then, faster and more often as nodes are destroyed.
Lights around one spot of the ship flicker or black out.
Nodes are radioactive: being close costs health, and radiation resistance counts. Larger nodes reach further.
The helmet light stutters near a node.
The growth disappears when the last node is destroyed.
Saving the settings file while the game is running now applies the change at once.

0.6.0
The atmosphere regulator's hiss and vent effect now last for the whole of a slowed pressure change. New settings: ExtendPressureEffects and PressureEffectSeconds.
This mod now only handles what is specific to the design of the MCRN Corvette ships: gentle part separation and room pressure times.
Removed: centering and straightening ships in the bay, and moving the jacks.

0.5.0
New GentleSeparation setting, on by default. On custom ships, heavy parts are no longer thrown off at speed when cut or yanked free.
The push between overlapping parts is limited by mass: 0.3 m/s for parts of 5 tonnes or more, the game's own speed for parts up to 100 kg.
The grapple, tethers, explosions and decompression are unchanged, and so are the game's own ships.

0.4.0
New StraightenShip setting, on by default. A custom ship posed at an angle is turned square to the bay before it is centered.
The ship's length comes from the tightest box around it, and its roll from its mirror symmetry where width and height are too alike.
New StraightenRatio setting, for how clear a ship's shape must be before it is trusted.
Saved ships load as they were saved: as posed, centered, or straightened and centered.

0.3.2
CenterShip is on by default, for custom ships only. The game's own ships and their jacks are never moved.

0.3.1
Better choice of placement when restoring a saved ship.
The Master Jack's scanner outline follows it to its new position.

0.3.0
New CenterShip setting. Places the ship in the middle of the bay and moves the jacks clear of it.
The settings file is re-read on every ship load.
Removed variant unlock, size thresholds and mass override.
